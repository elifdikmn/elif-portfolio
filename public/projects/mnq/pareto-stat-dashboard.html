1-Min Volume — Median, 1st SD & 2nd SD Upper (24h)
How to read: Bars = median volume per slot. Lines = 1st and 2nd standard deviation upper bounds. Volume exceeding the 1st SD line signals larger-than-normal price movement is more likely. The gold spike at 09:30 is the NYSE open — highest-volume minute of the day, every day.
30-Min Volume Median by Half-Hour
The 09:30 candle accounts for a disproportionate share of daily volume — typically 3–5× the next highest slot. Execution quality (spread, slippage) improves significantly after 10:00 as volume normalizes.
5-Second Range Bars — Median & 1st SD
Range bars completed per minute at each 30-min slot. A high count = price completing more micro-swings per unit time. Use as a real-time volatility proxy — above 1st SD means unusually fast tape.
Harmonic Rotation Stats — Full Session vs Opening Hour
A harmonic rotation is a confirmed swing from local high to local low (or vice versa), measured in points. These stats define the typical swing amplitude in each session — use for target and stop calibration.
1-Min Fractal Method — Session Rotation Count & Mean

Median — Full Session vs Opening Hour (pts)
Full Distribution: Mean / Median / P75 / P90
P75 = 75% of rotations smaller than this value. P90 = 90% smaller. Treat P90 as the maximum rotation on a typical day. Rotations exceeding P90 signal a potential regime shift — consider reducing size.
Full Session vs Opening Hour Probabilities
Full Session = close-to-close. Opening Hour = first 60 min only. These can diverge significantly — a session that opens down may close up after reversing. Check both timeframes before forming a bias.
